# ProInspect public website

## Register

brand

## Users

Western Australian property owners, landlords, professional property managers,
commercial owners, strata representatives, accommodation operators, project teams,
prospective renters and existing tenants. Visitors need to understand the relevant
service, find available rental properties or reach the appropriate account or enquiry
path without understanding the platform's internal workspaces.

## Product Purpose

Present ProInspect's property services clearly and persuasively, then connect each
visitor with the right next step. Public service pages explain the offering; verified
account and property relationships govern actual bookings, records and subscriptions.
The existing leasing catalogue, open-home registrations and rental applications remain
authoritative. The public site and authenticated application share one Worker and origin.

## Brand Personality

Assured, practical and welcoming. Preserve the supplied ProInspect identity and the
existing deep-teal, warm-gold and light-neutral presentation. The reference for this
refresh is the owner's current header screenshot and their request for a professional
real-estate presentation, useful service detail and a clearer route into the Client Portal.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy dashboard styling on public pages, repetitive generic service cards, thin
placeholder copy, fabricated testimonials, unsupported performance claims and images
presented as completed ProInspect jobs or rental listings without genuine provenance.
Do not add image captions saying Illustration; the owner has explicitly requested their
removal. Generated brand imagery must not substitute for real listing photographs.

## Design Principles

- Explain the service and its practical value before asking a visitor to act.
- Make the logo the Home link; keep the service and audience navigation easy to scan.
- Use a coherent image direction, generous spacing and consistent page structure.
- Preserve working leasing, contact, security and application flows rather than recreate them.
- Distinguish available services from planned management capabilities without making
  promises about prices, availability, professional approvals or future releases.
- Write natural, specific Western Australian property copy with descriptive headings,
  accurate canonical metadata and useful internal links instead of keyword stuffing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain the repository's WCAG 2.2 AA design target, semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable
navigation, visible focus, labelled forms, descriptive alternative text, reduced-motion
support and mobile reflow. Automated checks do not constitute formal conformance.

## Scope

This refresh covers public marketing and leasing presentation. Sign-in/sign-up redesign,
new account entitlements, billing activation, production data changes and legal-policy
approval are outside this work. Existing production deployment controls remain in place.
