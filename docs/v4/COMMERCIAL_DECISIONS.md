# Commercial decisions required before activating a V4 PAYG catalogue

| Decision | Documents state | Proposed release treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Residential weekly/monthly subscription | Combined inclusions: $30 + GST/week/property; updated residential fee schedule: $150 + GST/month/property | Neither is automatically applied; approve effective plan, grandfathering, inclusions and expiry. |
| Residential payment terms | Updated PAYG schedule: payable within seven days of service | Post-service invoice unless separately agreed; do not assume upfront Stripe capture. |
| Commercial payment terms | Commercial PAYG schedule does not define them | Require approval of invoicing/Checkout terms before purchase. |
| Fixed vs quoted | 45 residential fixed, 115 commercial fixed; POA/From/plus-cost other modes | Show reference amount only until scope and rate approved. POA requires Staff quotation. |
| Geographic fees | Commercial schedule: standard Perth & Peel; extra regional/urgent/after-hours charges may apply | Obtain approved geographic/rate matrix. No invented postcode charge. |
| Bundled components | Lease administration, records and maintenance packs overlap individually priced tasks | Approve parent/component charging map, prevent duplicate billing. |
| Third-party costs | Commercial schedule says contractors, statutory fees, equipment and disbursements additional | Obtain itemised customer approvals; never infer contractor fee. |
| Statutory documents | WA form catalogue and fee schedule include official legal forms | Legal source/version, grounds, timing, permission and method of service must be accepted before issue. |
| Confidential Form 2 | Listed at $100 but contains restricted family-violence information | Excluded from ordinary public catalogue and email. Dedicated restricted channel only. |
| Court paperwork | Residential schedule lists POA | Restricted Staff-controlled scope; not generic auto-filing. |
| Service licence/insurance | Legal/professional advice and regulated property activities are outside generic administrative scope | Confirm permitted role, required licence and professional review before offering. |
