# Customer account: all-inclusive subscription booking (working policy)

## Owner-confirmed assumption — 17 October 2026

**Every active customer account has an all-inclusive subscription giving access to
all service and platform features appropriate to its existing portal role and
authorised properties.** No additional service-level price or quote is required
to schedule an eligible service. The current working assumption imposes no
numerical cap on appointment count; actual booking is constrained by available
staff capacity, service duration, notice period, sector and lawful access.

"All features" **does not** create roles or cross-tenant privileges. Tenant,
Building, Council and Staff identities keep their existing roles and access
rules; they are not given separate customer billing accounts merely by signing
in. Property Manager, Strata Manager, Commercial and Landlord clients share the
same subscription model while retaining distinct workflows and workspaces.
Third-party charges, contractor materials and statutory fees are not made
automatically free by a service booking; those require their own recorded approval.

## Provisioning

Database migration `0006_all_inclusive_subscription.sql` provisions
`subscriptions` with `plan_code=all_inclusive`,
`status=active`, `property_id=NULL`,
`allowance_json={"includedServiceIds":["*"]}`.

1. Backfills **active client accounts without an existing subscription**.
2. Automatically provisions future clients when created as active.
3. Provisions clients transitioned from pending/inactive to active when they
   have no subscription.
4. Leaves existing subscription status and allowances intact, including
   paused/ended subscriptions; these cannot be silently reactivated.

This is a working entitlement model, **not evidence of a payment collected or
a Stripe subscription**. No recurring invoice or payment is generated by this
migration. A future paid-subscription contract should be integrated separately.

Migration execution on Cloudflare D1 is a **distinct operational step** from
Workers Builds deployment. Never assume that simply merging the code applies
the D1 schema/backfill.

## Direct booking

- All active, sector-compatible services are included for authorised client
  properties. A service marked `request` or `price_ex_gst_cents=NULL`
  in the global catalogue can still be instantly scheduled via a valid
  subscription.
- Display "Included — no additional charge" rather than "Quote required".
  The public catalogue price is not changed to $0.
- Availability checks workspace and property authorisation, then finds slots
  using the existing single-resource scheduler and overlap/block rules.
- Confirmation revalidates account, property, service and active plan. It
  records `bookings.price_ex_gst_cents=0` for the *additional visit charge*,
  plus `bookingBasis=subscription_included` and the plan identifier in the
  existing immutable booking snapshot.
- Booking confirmation creates the usual Work Order, confidential access
  envelope, audit event, operational notification and customer email. The
  existing notice, tenancy, scheme and access requirements are unchanged.
- Existing booked work remains visible after a subscription is paused.
  Paused/ended subscriptions cannot schedule new included work.
- Capacity rules still apply. A confirmed booking prevents double booking
  through the scheduler Durable Object and D1 overlap triggers.

## Stage 2 operational verification

The production account inventory on 17 October 2026 had four active,
synthetic customer clients and zero subscriptions. Deploy only after GitHub
integration, database migration/schema checks, browser acceptance and
Wrangler dry-runs pass.

1. Apply the approved D1 migration and verify exactly one
   `all_inclusive` subscription per previously-unsubscribed active test client.
2. Verify the existing Landlord can book a synthetic PCR directly for
   `prop_pi_test_landlord`, with no quote and no additional payment.
3. Verify a canonical booking and Work Order are created, and the Staff
   portal sees the same Work Order and corresponding notifications.
4. Verify a different client cannot access the same property or use the
   other account's subscription.
5. Verify cancellation, rescheduling, duplicate submission and any
   appointment conflict behaviour.
6. Verify the other authorised client workspaces can schedule their
   sector-compatible included services without gaining extra roles.
7. A normal subscriber should not be able to change another account's
   subscription or approval status.

**Production safety:** Do not manufacture a live booking or Work Order
without authorising the exact synthetic service and appointment. Do not
reconfigure DNS, payment secrets or email delivery for this change.
