# ProInspect Residential Property Support — Commercial terms draft

**Status: internal legal/commercial review only. Not yet a customer agreement.**

## Parties and authority
The subscriber is the verified owner of the residential property or an authorised individual responsible for its self-management. ProInspect provides inspection, attendance, documentation and operational coordination services within the approved scope. The subscriber retains management/legal responsibility and must give lawful instructions, access arrangements and relevant tenant notices. Activities requiring a licensed property agent or legal adviser are outside this contract unless an authorised professional is separately engaged.

## Subscription price
AUD $50 excluding GST per residential property each week. Assuming 10% Australian GST applies, AUD $55 including GST per property each week. A different number of properties results in a proportional charge only where their individual subscriptions have independently started.

## When billing begins
The subscription is created through Stripe-hosted Checkout, with the first recurring invoice scheduled for the next eligible Friday at 9:00 am Australia/Perth time. Checkout must collect a usable payment method. ProInspect does not advertise a free trial; the unpaid interval before the first Friday charge does not provide included-service entitlements. Stripe does not prorate or charge immediately at signup. Friday billing times are subject to payment network processing and invoice finalisation timing.

## Renewal and included services
Each successfully settled weekly invoice grants access to applicable subscription-covered services for the paid weekly period. Work remains subject to eligibility, property authorisation, scheduling, exclusions and applicable law. Active subscription status alone does not establish a paid entitlement.

## Payment failure and recovery
Stripe automatic retries are requested. An existing subscriber with a verified prior paid invoice receives a configurable provisional 72-hour grace period from the previous paid-through time, subject to final approval. New subscribers without a successful first payment receive no grace entitlement. Risk events, refunds, unresolved disputes, cancellation or confirmed fraud can suspend entitlements immediately. Stripe Smart Retries/dunning settings must be verified in the live Stripe Dashboard.

## Cancellation
Customers may cancel in the protected Stripe Customer Portal or by following the authorised support channel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid weekly period; no further weekly recurring invoice is intended after that period. Service access stops at the end of the paid period, subject to law.

## Refunds
No automatic partial-week refund applies to a voluntary cancellation. This does **not** exclude, restrict or replace any statutory guarantees or refunds owed under Australian Consumer Law, and ProInspect may approve additional discretionary exceptions. Where a refund is issued, access and supporting records will be reconciled.

## Service scope, conditions and exclusions
The separate Residential Subscription Schedule must specify the exact services, number/frequency of visits, available suburbs/times, reporting turnaround, 24-hour urgent works attendance conditions, third-party contractor/material costs and all conditional “Included*” items. No unconditional guarantees should be made before these are settled. Specialist forms, court documents, family-violence records and legal procedures require appropriate authority, security and legal review. Contractor charges, repairs, travel and external fees may not be covered unless explicitly stated.

## Notices, records and privacy
ProInspect may send receipts, account notices, failed-payment reminders and cancellation communications. Privacy, data retention, access restrictions and sensitive tenancy information remain subject to the approved privacy policy and statutory obligations.

## Release sign-off required
1. Accountant-approved Product Tax Code replacing downloadable-software code.
2. Approved 72-hour or alternative grace period.
3. Published availability and exclusions for every Included* service.
4. Live Stripe retry/dunning settings and billing disclosure verified.
5. Independent legal review for Australian Consumer Law and relevant WA tenancy compliance.
6. Signed hosted production acceptance for online identity, property enrollment, Friday invoice processing, cancellation and data recovery.

This draft is not automatically accepted or published to customers; versioned legal acceptance in ProInspect is required for the final approved terms.

## Existing client migration safety
Historic all-inclusive properties are explicitly grandfathered and cannot be auto-enrolled into a new live paid subscription by opening Checkout. Only properties created through the separately approved paid-onboarding release may start live Checkout. A reviewed, voluntary conversion path for existing property records requires independent commercial, legal, billing and entitlement acceptance before implementation; never suspend historic services merely because a subscriber clicks an incomplete payment session.
