## Communications continuation

The contact route preserves service intent and returns a reference without granting property access. The marketing Worker authenticates its server-to-server payload. Hosted submissions require separate Turnstile hostname/action validation. Operations staff have immutable enquiry history and version-guarded assignment, state, reply and client-reference actions.

Notice dispatch claims at most five jobs and 25 recipients per job. Stable delivery IDs and a transaction-bound cursor prevent duplicate fan-out. Expired/withdrawn notices and revoked memberships suppress pending mail. Council-only notice content requires the Council workspace even when the same person is a resident. Retry affects failed work only; sent/suppressed receipts are not recreated. Historical notices are not emailed by migration.

See COMMUNICATIONS.md for configuration and delivery semantics. Cloudflare remains locked. The old repositories and all existing hosted resources are unchanged.
