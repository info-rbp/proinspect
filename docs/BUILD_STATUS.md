The new portals are permission-backed operational workspaces, not public role selectors. A professional application cannot approve itself. Owning a lot does not confer council authority. A tenant/resident can use both workspaces under one identity without permission union. The old repositories and all existing hosted resources remain unchanged.

## Continuity implementation notes

The booking-to-report, professional portfolio, strata, tenant and Staff workflows remain on the same canonical records. This increment adds migrations `0004_communications.sql` and `0005_payment_attempts.sql`; it does not rewrite the frozen identity or property model.

Workspace document links now call `/api/w/:kind/:scopeId/documents/:documentId/download`. Lists and these endpoints evaluate the active workspace, not all roles held by the person. The legacy `/api/documents/:id/download` remains an authenticated personal archive endpoint with its existing identity-level grants; it is not used by the workspace document screens.

Scheduled notices deliver once per notice version/person. All-member notices choose one eligible workspace for a person rather than sending duplicate notifications for overlapping resident/owner/council roles. Only verified active users are delivery recipients; members invited after the publication date may read currently applicable notices, but are not sent historical announcement emails. Notice text is not included in email. A revocation, expiry, withdrawal or version change suppresses an obsolete queued email and is recorded as `NOTICE_NO_LONGER_AVAILABLE`, not as a successful send.

Checkout success URLs never mark a record paid. An unpaid checkout completion moves the ledger to pending; a signed asynchronous success/failure or expiry drives the next state. Each provider session remains in attempt history. Conflicting or superseded settlements appear in the Staff payment-reconciliation queue. Recording a reviewed reference does not perform a refund or charge. An elapsed checkout timestamp alone never opens a second payment while its provider outcome is unknown.

All deployment workflows remain locked. Repository acceptance runs local Workers bindings and provider doubles only, never live customer payments or external email delivery.
