# Launch scope and delivery order

The target includes all discussed workspaces and workflows; an unimplemented tab is not a delivered capability.

1. Workers skeleton and exact-source verification.
2. Shared domain, relationship authorization, identity, design system.
3. Landlord booking-to-report vertical slice including staff counterpart.
4. Tenancy requests, document requests, approvals and contextual notifications.
5. Property-manager teams, portfolio scope, safe imports, bulk scheduling and recurring reminders.
6. Strata scheme/building/lot relationships, manager operations, resident requests/notices, council oversight.
7. Commercial/asset workflow using the portfolio engine.
8. Versioned WA form workflows and restricted evidence; require current form review and explicit isolation acceptance before enablement.
9. Report Tool round trip, provider-backed payments and Sheets projection.
10. Migration rehearsal and production acceptance.

Marketing includes service/sector/engagement pages, proof and resource templates, enquiries and contextual booking CTAs. No invented prices, client testimonials, availability or legal claims. Authentication email is a core dependency, not a deferred integration. Manual report upload and manual payment records are acceptable initial working capabilities, but not evidence that external integrations are complete.

Deferred: native apps, AI features, strata accounting/levies, statutory voting, social feeds, parcel management, unreviewed mass migration and one microservice per portal.
