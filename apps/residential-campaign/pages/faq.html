Eligibility and coverage

Who can use this residential subscription?

It is designed for owners who self-manage an eligible residential rental property and can give lawful instructions. Professional agents may need a different arrangement.

Which suburbs and property types are covered?

Service capacity is confirmed by individual property address, type, access and requested work. This preview does not publish an approved complete suburb list. Check service eligibility.

Can I subscribe for multiple properties?

ProInspect supports property portfolios. The intended price is $50 + GST for each eligible residential property every paid week; each address and service arrangement needs to be assessed.

Services and inspections

What does the weekly subscription include?

The current service schedule names 48 activities covering condition reporting, quarterly routine inspections, leasing assistance, access, maintenance coordination and WA tenancy document support. Four items have additional conditions. Read the full list.

Does ProInspect replace my property manager?

Not automatically. The landlord remains responsible for decisions and services outside the agreed subscription scope, including rent collection and tenant selection where not separately arranged.

Are routine inspections included?

Quarterly routine inspection activity is listed as included, subject to lawful entry, access arrangements, schedule conditions and availability. Further visits may require authorisation.

Are contractor labour and repair materials covered by $50 + GST?

Do not assume so. Coordination and specified inspection activity appear in the service schedule, while third-party labour, materials, repairs and specialist services may be separate costs requiring approval.

Can ProInspect prepare tenancy forms?

The schedule includes administrative assistance with named WA forms. The landlord or relevant adviser must check whether a notice or legal step is suitable and valid; ProInspect does not provide legal representation.

Can I download a sample PCR?

Approved privacy-cleared reports are not yet available for download. The sample reports page shows clearly labelled illustrative layouts only.

Pricing, payments and cancellation

How much is the subscription?

The intended price is $50 plus GST ($55 total at 10% GST) per eligible residential property per week. Other costs may apply outside the defined service scope.

When will payments be taken?

The planned recurring payment is every Friday at 9:00 am Perth time. Live recurring Checkout is not yet enabled; there is currently no live weekly charge via this campaign.

When do paid services begin?

Under the intended model, paid-service access begins after a verified successful first invoice, not just because an account has been created.

Can I cancel?

The intended cancellation takes effect at the end of the already paid weekly period. No automatic partial-week refund is planned, subject always to statutory rights and approved exceptions.

What happens when a payment fails?

Automatic payment retries and a grace period before suspension are planned, but the final live retry and grace policy still needs approval. See pricing and billing.

Registration and privacy

Do I need to create a new password on this site?

No. This promotional site links to ProInspect's existing secure account system for one-time email sign-in and Residential Landlord onboarding.

Is my card charged when I register an account?

No card information is collected on this marketing site. Account setup is separate from a live paid subscription, which is not yet enabled.

Where can I review privacy and terms?

The subscription terms overview explains current draft product principles, while the main ProInspect website hosts the general privacy policy and terms.
