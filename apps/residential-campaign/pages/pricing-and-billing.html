Cancellations and payment difficulties

What happens if plans or payments change?

These are the current intended policy principles, provided for planning. The approved customer agreement and mandatory Australian consumer rights will govern when subscriptions go live.

01

Cancellation timing

The planned approach is cancellation at the end of the already paid weekly period, rather than immediately removing paid access.

View the terms overview →
02

Refunds

There is no automatic partial-week refund simply because a customer cancels. Applicable legal rights, errors and approved exceptions remain available.

Ask a billing question →
03

Failed payments

Automatic retries are intended through Stripe Billing. A grace period is planned before service suspension, but the duration and final reminder policy require approval.
