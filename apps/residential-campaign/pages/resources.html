Check current Western Australian guidance.

Residential tenancy rules can change. For the legal requirements that apply to notices, entries, maintenance and bonds, check official Western Australian consumer protection resources rather than relying on a general marketing summary.

Before acting on any guide

  • Confirm the facts of your own tenancy and written agreements.
  • Check current statutory forms, required notices and timelines.
  • Obtain professional advice if legal rights or obligations are unclear.
  • Keep records of decisions, permissions and communication.
