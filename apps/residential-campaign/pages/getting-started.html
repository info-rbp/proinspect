Frequently asked questions

Registration and getting started

Do I need an existing ProInspect account?

No. The secure sign-in flow also starts new-user registration through email verification.

Who can use the Residential Landlord account?

The current onboarding flow is for people who own and self-manage the residential property being registered. Professional managers administering other people's properties use a separate arrangement.

Can I register multiple rental properties?

Yes, the ProInspect platform supports multiple authorised properties, but subscriptions must be linked and accepted per property's billing and service eligibility rules.

Is my card charged when I create an account?

No. Creating an account is separate from Stripe Checkout. The promotional microsite does not collect cards. Live Friday collection remains disabled pending product tax classification, webhook security and independent acceptance.
