What the subscription delivers

Practical help. Better visibility. Your decisions stay yours.

ProInspect is designed to support owners who manage their own rental property. The value is in structured attendance, communication and records across the tenancy — not a claim that all property-management responsibilities disappear.

01 / DOCUMENT

Know what is happening

Condition reports, routine visits, photographs where relevant and follow-up observations help turn individual visits into an accessible property history.

02 / ORGANISE

Help at every tenancy stage

Preparation before leasing, open-home attendance, move-in support, renewal administration and outgoing inspections all sit within the listed service scope.

03 / COORDINATE

Follow up without losing context

Maintenance intake, contractor coordination and targeted follow-up reports help keep authorised work and related communications together.

04 / STAY IN CONTROL

Keep the landlord in charge

You retain your authority over spending, tenant decisions and tenancy obligations. ProInspect provides defined operational assistance and a record of instructed tasks.

Common questions

Understand what you're signing up for.

Does ProInspect replace my property manager?

Not automatically. The residential subscription is designed for landlords who self-manage. It provides defined inspections, attendance, coordination and document support. You remain responsible for rent collection, tenant decisions, legal obligations and matters outside the agreed scope.

Are quarterly routine inspections included?

Yes. Quarterly routine inspections are listed in the subscription schedule, including the relevant entry notice, visit, report, tenant communication and follow-up, subject to lawful entry and agreed terms. Additional follow-up inspections are also listed.

Are contractors and repair materials included in $50 + GST?

No blanket inclusion should be assumed. The subscription lists maintenance coordination and specified visits, but contractor costs, materials, specialist repairs and the extent of any physical works require agreement and authorised expenditure.

Can ProInspect help with leasing and tenant changeover?

The schedule includes open-home attendance, a property walkthrough video, social media marketing, application administration, condition reporting, move-in support, renewal administration and vacate support. Listing costs outside that scope and decisions about accepting tenants remain separate.

Are WA tenancy forms and notices prepared for me?

The schedule names administrative assistance with common WA tenancy forms. The landlord or responsible adviser must review content and decide whether a notice or process is lawful and appropriate; ProInspect does not claim to provide legal advice or representation.

How does the $50 + GST weekly subscription work?

The stated price is $50 plus 10% GST ($55 total) per eligible residential property per week. Friday billing in Perth time is the planned schedule. Registration, billing and included-service access remain subject to the approved terms and production activation.
