Common questions

A few answers before you begin.

Who is this service designed for?

Owners of eligible residential rental properties who self-manage and want defined assistance with inspections, leasing administration, access, reporting and maintenance coordination. It is not automatically full tenancy management.

Are trades and repairs included in the $50 fee?

Do not assume so. The schedule includes defined coordination, administrative tasks and visits. Third-party contractor invoices, parts, specialist services and physical work require approval and agreed terms. Four services are conditional.

Can I start paying for the subscription today?

Residential Landlord account registration is available through ProInspect. Live recurring Checkout remains disabled pending approval. Once activated, the planned price is $50 + GST per eligible property per week, with Friday billing and service access after confirmed first payment.
