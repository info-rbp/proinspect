Where we can support you

Support starts with the right property.

We help self-managing residential landlords arrange defined inspections and property support. Before relying on availability, confirm that your address, property type and access arrangements fall within our current service capacity.

Three things determine eligibility.

The subscription is intended for an eligible, owner-managed residential rental property with sufficient authority and practical access for the requested work.

01

Service location

Availability is assessed against the specific property address, travel requirements, inspection capacity and the work requested. We have not published an approved suburb-by-suburb coverage list.

What happens before your first service?

Registration and service eligibility are related but different. An account can be created without guaranteeing that every service can be delivered at a particular address.

Eligibility questions

What if my property needs something different?

Do you cover every Perth suburb?

We cannot confirm universal coverage from the current approved materials. Contact ProInspect with your property's address for an availability assessment before scheduling work.

Can I register an apartment or strata property?

Residential units may have additional access, strata and authority requirements. Property eligibility and attendance arrangements should be confirmed for the particular address.

Can an agency register on behalf of an owner?

This residential product is positioned for owners who self-manage. A professional property manager administering third-party properties may need a different ProInspect service arrangement.

Does eligibility guarantee that every service is available immediately?

No. Actual visits remain subject to the agreed scope, staff availability, lawful entry, property access and any service-specific conditions.
