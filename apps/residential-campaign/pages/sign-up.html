Before subscribing

Clear responsibility, clear terms.

Residential Landlord registration is intended for owners who manage their own property. The service arrangement needs clear instructions and accepted scope.

Your property and authority

You'll be asked to confirm that you own and self-manage the properties registered to your account.

Billing and contracted services

Live Stripe recurring billing and payment-dependent entitlement activation are still gated. Any full paid subscription launch requires an approved commercial and legal release.

A final note

Know what you're registering for.

Does registering automatically start paid billing?

No. The existing account setup and live recurring payment collection are separate systems at present. The Stripe subscription implementation is sandbox-only, and live collection has not been activated.

Can I get started if I have more than one property?

Yes. ProInspect supports property portfolios. After verifying your email, add eligible properties through the landlord workspace. The number of properties is reflected in subscription pricing once the approved billing implementation is enabled.

Where are my personal information and payment details handled?

Your account identity and property information are handled by ProInspect's existing secure platform. This promotional website does not collect personal details or payment card numbers.
