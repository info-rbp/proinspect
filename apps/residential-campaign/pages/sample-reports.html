Questions about inspection reports

What the examples do — and don't — show.

These demonstration layouts illustrate a reporting approach. They do not establish report delivery times, guarantee photographs for every observation, or replace the final approved ProInspect templates.

01

Evidence rather than assumptions

Reports describe what was observed at a particular visit. They are not building certifications, exhaustive safety audits or statements about hidden defects.

Inspection details →
02

Relevant supporting photographs

Photographs can support a condition record when available and appropriate. Privacy, consent, access and the actual approved template determine what may be shown.
