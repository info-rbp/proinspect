Plain-language scope overview

Understand the agreement before you accept it.

This page summarises the intended Residential Property Support arrangement in general language. It is not the final legally approved subscription contract or a substitute for the agreement presented during secure onboarding.

What the arrangement covers

The main matters customers should understand.

These themes help explain the service and where the owner, an authorised professional or a separate contractor remains responsible.

01

Who can subscribe

The product is designed for eligible residential property owners who self-manage and can give lawful instructions, property details and access authority.

Check eligibility →
02

Included service scope

Inspections, reporting, leasing administration, keys, maintenance coordination and WA form assistance are described in the 48-item service schedule. Four items have additional conditions.

Read the service schedule →
03

Owner decisions and legal duties

The owner or appointed licensed professional remains responsible for tenant selection, rent collection, legal decisions and statutory compliance unless a separate lawful arrangement says otherwise.

Read responsibility FAQs →
04

External costs and limits

Contractor labour, materials, repairs, certain advertising or third-party charges, and work beyond agreed limits may need separate authorisation and payment.

Review exclusions →
05

Property attendance and access

Inspections and key-related activity depend on lawful entry, notices, safety, tenant coordination, availability and approved access arrangements.

See access requirements →
06

Price and recurring billing

The intended subscription is $50 plus 10% GST per eligible property per week, with charges scheduled on Fridays at 9:00 am Perth time after billing approval.

Read billing details →
07

Cancellation and refunds

The intended cancellation takes effect at the end of a paid weekly period. No automatic partial-week credit applies, subject always to consumer rights and approved exceptions.

Read cancellation principles →
08

Privacy and service records

Identity, property records and any payment arrangements are handled in the protected ProInspect application; this marketing site is informational and does not collect payment details.

Privacy information →

Before entering an agreement

Review the exact inclusions, permissions and payment terms.

The approved agreement should make clear which services are scheduled, what additional visits or external costs may require authorisation, who may give instructions, how payment failures are handled, and how consumer-law rights are preserved.

Items still requiring formal approval

  • Final residential subscription agreement and incorporated service schedule.
  • Conditions for the four starred inclusions.
  • Coverage, attendance limits and exceptional service fees.
  • Failed-payment notice and grace-period rules.
  • Complaint handling, service disputes and consumer-rights wording.

Where to read more

The broader ProInspect website also publishes general service terms and a privacy policy. Those pages do not replace the need for an approved residential product agreement.

Can I accept this summary as the contract?

No. This is explanatory draft content, not an executed contract. An approved version of the product agreement must be presented and accepted using the designated secure workflow.

Does the subscription replace a managing agent?

Not automatically. ProInspect supplies defined property support services; the owner retains responsibilities not expressly delegated under an appropriate arrangement.
