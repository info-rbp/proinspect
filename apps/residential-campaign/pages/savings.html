Lower fees don't mean identical services.

Assumptions and references

Built around a transparent 24-month scenario.

The 24-month term is a modelling benchmark, not a newly verified average WA tenancy length for 2026. The fee schedule is an illustrative scenario supplied for this campaign; actual real estate agreements can bundle, waive or change charges.

The model assumes a continuously occupied property, unchanged rent, one tenant over the selected period, one letting at the start, and one final exit. It excludes vacancy losses, repairs, council rates, strata, insurance, finance, income-tax effects and the value of the landlord's own time. There is no assumed portfolio discount.

Research behind the starting figures

$750/week rental example: REIWA's September 2026 Perth median house rent data.

Routine inspections: WA Consumer Protection permits no more than four routine inspections per year. This model assumes three agency inspections annually by default.

Agency fees: 9% + GST commission, letting fees and separate charges are illustrative amounts from the supplied cost analysis, not independently verified market-wide averages.
